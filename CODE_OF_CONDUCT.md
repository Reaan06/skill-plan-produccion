# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous community. Participants must:

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing evidence and trade-offs.
- Write constructively and welcome different backgrounds and viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas, behavior, and artifacts—not people.
- Protect privacy and avoid sharing secrets, personal data, or private project information.
- Follow the repository's security-reporting boundary.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner, [Reaan06](https://github.com/Reaan06), or a private GitHub channel requested from that profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when this standard is violated.
